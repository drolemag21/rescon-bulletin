What
: Colorado's Office of Information Technology says hackers breached the myColorado app, found on November 18. Residents use it for digital ID and state services. The state shut the app down and says data on about 150,000 people was likely stolen.

Who
: Unknown. The state has not attributed the attack.

Status
: The app was still offline on November 24. Officials have not said what data was taken. Services remain available through agency websites.
