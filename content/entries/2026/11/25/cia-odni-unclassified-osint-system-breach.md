What
: Hackers breached an unclassified open-source intelligence analysis system used by the CIA and ODNI. The New York Times first reported it on November 24. CyberScoop and AP confirmed it through people familiar with the incident.

Who
: Not attributed. The New York Times and Nextgov report the intruders allegedly used Artex, a Chinese-made AI intrusion platform.

Status
: The Times reports the NGA was also affected. Sources say classified networks were not. What data was exposed is unclear.
