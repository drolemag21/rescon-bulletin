What
: FinCEN, the Treasury's financial intelligence unit, said on November 24 that attackers accessed some of its systems. It took systems offline as a precaution and extended filing deadlines.

Who
: Unknown. No one has claimed or been blamed for the intrusion.

Status
: A spokesperson told The Record the FBI and CISA are investigating. Investigators see no sign so far that the Bank Secrecy Act or beneficial ownership databases were breached.
