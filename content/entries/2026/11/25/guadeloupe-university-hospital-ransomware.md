What
: A cyberattack starting November 21 encrypted systems at the University Hospital of Guadeloupe, in the French Caribbean. Surgeries and chemotherapy were postponed and radiotherapy suspended. France's cyber agency ANSSI suspects ransomware.

Who
: Unknown. No group has claimed it, and the hospital says it has received no ransom demand.

Status
: ANSSI is examining whether entry came through a compromised IT supplier. Prosecutors opened an investigation. Recovery is expected to take several days.
