What
: South Korea's state power utility KEPCO says attackers reached its KEPCO ON billing platform and its procurement system. It confirmed the access on November 20. Data on over 1.5 million customers, employees and contractors was exposed.

Who
: Unknown. An actor using the alias Noiem advertised KEPCO data on a forum; that claim is not verified.

Status
: KEPCO blocked the access, suspended affected services and is notifying people. It cited AI-assisted techniques but gave no details.
