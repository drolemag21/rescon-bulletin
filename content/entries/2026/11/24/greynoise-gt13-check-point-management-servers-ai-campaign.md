What
: GreyNoise reports a campaign since early October that held access to 24 Check Point Security Management servers at 22 organizations. Victims include energy, government, finance and transport firms in the US, UK, Australia and Europe.

Who
: An actor GreyNoise tracks as GT13. It says GT13 combines agentic tooling with hands-on activity. No state link has been reported.

Status
: How the servers were first breached is unknown. GreyNoise believes access persists.
