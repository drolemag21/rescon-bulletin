What
: Australian uranium producer Peninsula Energy told the ASX that a cyber incident on Friday night breached the "operating system" at its Lance Project in Wyoming. It said operations will be temporarily affected. Administrative systems were reportedly untouched.

Who
: Unknown. No attribution has been reported.

Status
: Peninsula requested a trading halt until it issues a fuller announcement, or until 25 November at the latest. It has not said whether production stopped.
