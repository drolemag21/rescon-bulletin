What
: The UK NCSC says AI-assisted attacks have risen markedly since September. Organisations across several sectors report automated exploitation of internet-facing services, causing downtime or compromise.

Who
: Various threat actors using repurposed red-team frameworks. The Record reports that the NCSC names Artex and five others.

Status
: The NCSC stresses that the weaknesses exploited are not new. It urges basic hygiene, noting that the frameworks are currently noisy and detectable.
