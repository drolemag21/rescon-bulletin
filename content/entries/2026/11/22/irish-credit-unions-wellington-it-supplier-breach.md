What
: Attackers exploited a flaw at Wellington IT, a Belfast supplier of credit union mobile banking apps, in early October. Ireland's data regulator has received over 40 breach notifications, and UK reports are starting.

Who
: Unknown. No actor has been named.

Status
: Payee details of app users were exposed. Ireland's government says there is no sign members' funds were touched. Credit unions were not told until November 16.
