What
: Microsoft says state-sponsored and criminal actors have built the ARTEX agentic attack framework into their tooling. They use it for reconnaissance, credential attacks and web exploitation.

Who
: Microsoft names China's Salt Typhoon and Crystal Typhoon, North Korea's Onyx Sleet and Pearl Sleet, and two Storm-tracked groups.

Status
: Microsoft says behavioural detection of machine-speed request bursts is the most reliable signal. A Chinese firm now sells a commercial version.
