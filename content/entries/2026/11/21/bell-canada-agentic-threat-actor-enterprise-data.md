What
: Bell Canada told BleepingComputer that an attacker reached several systems on November 17, including one used to order and provision circuits. Enterprise customer contacts, ticket titles and some network-element IP addresses were taken.

Who
: Unknown. Bell called it an "agentic threat actor", suggesting AI-driven tools. It says it does not know who is behind it.

Status
: Bell says customer services were unaffected. It is sharing indicators with enterprise customers and Canada's Cyber Centre, but not publicly.
