What
: Amazon found an exposed instance of the Cairn attack framework whose AI agents attacked East Asian universities in September. The agents found a zero-day in MONAI Label, a medical-imaging tool, and used it at two universities.

Who
: Amazon attributes it to an unnamed China-nexus actor with moderate confidence, based on language, time zone and targeting.

Status
: MONAI Label's developers published an advisory. CISA added the flaw to its KEV catalog on November 23. The universities and their country were not named.
