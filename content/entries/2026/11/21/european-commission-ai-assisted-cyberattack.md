What
: A Commission spokesperson said attackers used an AI tool in an intrusion detected on October 22. Several applications were taken offline and most are restored. SecurityWeek, citing Politico, says HR and translation systems were down for over 10 days.

Who
: Not attributed. The spokesperson knew of no ransom demand.

Status
: The investigation continues. The Commission says it has found no evidence that staff data was breached.
