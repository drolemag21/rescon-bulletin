What
: CNN reported on November 20 that the FBI is investigating an apparent intrusion into the National Crime Information Center, citing two people briefed on it. Police nationwide use NCIC for warrants, stolen property and background checks.

Who
: Unknown. BleepingComputer sees no sign of a link to ShinyHunters, who claimed an earlier FBI breach.

Status
: The FBI has not confirmed it. When and how it happened, and whether data was taken, is unclear.
