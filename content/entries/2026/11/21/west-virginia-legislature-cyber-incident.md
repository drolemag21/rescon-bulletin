What
: West Virginia's state legislature lost its website and disabled some IT systems after a cyberattack that began on November 19, The Record reports. A House of Delegates official said outside specialists are investigating.

Who
: Unknown. No group has claimed it, and officials did not say whether it was ransomware.

Status
: Officials say it appears limited to the legislature's own systems. No other state agencies are affected.
