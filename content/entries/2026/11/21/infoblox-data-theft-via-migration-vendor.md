What
: Networking and DNS security firm Infoblox confirmed that attackers reached company information through a compromised third-party data migration vendor. SecurityWeek, citing The Register, says stolen files included some customers' NIOS configuration snapshots.

Who
: Unknown.

Status
: Infoblox says it has no sign that its products, cloud services, software updates or build processes were affected.
