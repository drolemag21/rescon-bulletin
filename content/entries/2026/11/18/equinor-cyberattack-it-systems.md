What
: Equinor detected a cyberattack on some IT systems on November 17 and worked with Norwegian security authorities to contain it. On November 20 it said the attack had been stopped.

Who
: Not attributed. Equinor says the attack does not appear to have specifically targeted it.

Status
: Production ran normally, with no sign of stolen or altered data. Norway's NSM says Equinor and Skagerak Energi were hit through an exploited Azure API Management flaw.
