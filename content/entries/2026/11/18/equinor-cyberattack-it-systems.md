Equinor, Norway's state-controlled oil and gas company, said on November 17 that it had detected an ongoing cyberattack on certain IT systems and is working with Norwegian national security authorities to neutralise it; it said operations and production were not affected and it has seen no sign of data theft. In a November 18 update it said this was not a virus attack, so there is no risk of it spreading, that it now better understands how the attack was carried out and which systems were hit, and that it is too early to say how long restoring them will take. The Record, citing Reuters, reported that internal email was down; no group has been named or has claimed responsibility, and Norway raised its national cyber risk level in October after several other incidents this year.
