Equinor, Norway's state-controlled oil and gas company, said it detected a cyberattack on its IT systems on November 17 and is working with Norwegian national security authorities to contain it; employees may have trouble reaching corporate applications, and Reuters reported internal email was down. The company said oil and gas production was not affected and it has seen no sign of data theft, but it has not said how the attackers got in or how long recovery will take. No group has been named or has claimed responsibility; The Record notes the attack follows several other incidents in Norway this year, after which the country raised its national cyber risk level in October.
