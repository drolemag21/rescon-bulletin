Romania's Intelligence Service (SRI) said it detected an attempt to compromise an unnamed government institution in December 2025 and stopped it, attributing the attack to Turla, the long-running espionage group linked to Russia's FSB, which exploited Microsoft software vulnerabilities. SRI said the affected email service was restored and no exfiltration of sensitive data was found. The statement followed ESET research describing a new Turla toolkit configured to collect the Romanian target's email with NATO and EU institutions; Russia's embassy in Bucharest has denied the accusation.
