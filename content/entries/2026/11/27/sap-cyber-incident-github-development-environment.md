What
: SAP confirmed a cyber incident after Der Spiegel reported that hackers stole employee tokens for its GitHub development environment. That access reportedly allowed copying and changing source code.

Who
: Not attributed. SAP has not said how the attackers got in.

Status
: SAP says it found no evidence of access to customer business data. It is unclear whether any altered code reached customers.
