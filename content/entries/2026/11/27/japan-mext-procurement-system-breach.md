What
: Japan's education ministry (MEXT) disclosed on November 25 that hackers breached its government procurement system. It detected the intrusion on October 1. About 164,000 business records and 1,700 email addresses may have been exposed.

Who
: Not attributed. The ministry has not said how the attackers got in.

Status
: The system has been cut off from internal and external networks. The ministry says it has seen no sign the data was published or misused.
