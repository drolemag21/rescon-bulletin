What
: The UK Department for Work and Pensions took some online services offline on November 27 after a cyber incident. The Record quoted a department spokesperson.

Who
: Not attributed. The department has not described the nature of the incident.

Status
: Regular benefit and pension payments are not affected, the spokesperson said. The department is investigating with the NCSC and the Government Cyber Coordination Centre.
