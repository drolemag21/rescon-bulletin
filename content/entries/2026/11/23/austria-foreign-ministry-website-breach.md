What
: Austria's Foreign Ministry says hackers breached a provider-run website on November 12–13. They reached official contact details of staff in Vienna and at missions abroad. The ministry says the breach is contained.

Who
: Not attributed. The Record notes no public evidence links Russia, which Vienna blamed for a 2019–20 ministry intrusion.

Status
: Unclear how attackers got in or whether data was taken. National cyber and data protection authorities were notified.
