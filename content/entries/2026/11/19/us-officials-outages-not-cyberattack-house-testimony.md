What
: At a House Homeland Security hearing on November 18, top intelligence, DHS and FBI officials said recent outages were not a coordinated attack. These included a Microsoft outage, an FAA ground stop, banking glitches and an NSA network failure.

Who
: Not attributed. The FBI found no sign of malicious cyber activity.

Status
: Officials cited upgrades, patching and aging systems. The NSA said on November 20 it was back to normal after PHP patching disrupted its network.
