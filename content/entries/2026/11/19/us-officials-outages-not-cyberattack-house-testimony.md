What
: At a House Homeland Security hearing on November 18, the DNI, DHS Secretary and FBI Deputy Director said recent outages were not a coordinated attack. They included a Microsoft outage, an FAA ground stop, banking glitches and an NSA network failure, The Record reports.

Who
: Not attributed. The FBI said it has found no indication of malicious cyber activity.

Status
: Officials cited upgrades, patching and aging systems. The FBI is still checking with DHS and ODNI.
