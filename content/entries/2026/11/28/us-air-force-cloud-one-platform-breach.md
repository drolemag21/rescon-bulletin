What
: Hackers reportedly breached Cloud One, the Air Force cloud platform hosting Defense Department applications, Reuters reported and BleepingComputer relayed. The intrusion was found within the past month during a platform upgrade. A source says an unidentified software flaw was exploited.

Who
: Not attributed. The attackers have not been identified publicly.

Status
: US Cyber Command is reportedly assessing the impact. Data theft is unconfirmed, and the Air Force declined to comment.
